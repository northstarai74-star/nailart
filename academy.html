Curriculum

Sixteen weeks, in order.

This is the full Master & Business path. Foundation is weeks 1–4; the Artist Diploma is weeks 1–8.

01–02

Foundations & sanitation

Nail anatomy, product chemistry, contraindications, dry manicure and cuticle work. The unglamorous skills every set is built on.

TheoryPractical
03–04

Gel polish & structured overlay

Clean application and removal, then building a real apex on the natural plate — the difference between a four-day set and a four-week one.

Foundation ends
05–06

E-file mastery

Brushless machine work: bit selection, speed control, safe reduction. You'll unlearn hand-filing habits here.

Live models begin
07–08

Soft-gel & sculpted extensions

Tip application, sculpting forms, shaping to almond, coffin, squoval and stiletto with symmetry across all ten nails.

Diploma ends
09–11

Freehand art

One-stroke florals, fine-line work, marbling, chrome and encapsulation. Speed and repeatability, not just a single good nail.

Studio brushes
12–13

Editorial, bridal & competition

Press-on sets to spec, trial-and-final bridal workflow, and preparing a competition-standard set against the clock.

Guest artists
14–15

Business, pricing & retention

Costing a set properly, setting prices you won't have to undo, booking systems, and the client conversations that build a waitlist.

Classroom
16

Portfolio shoot & certification

A styled shoot of your final sets for your portfolio, final assessment, and your accredited certificate.

Master ends
