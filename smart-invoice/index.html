FAQ

How do automatic follow-ups work?

You choose a schedule, such as 3 days before, on the due date, and 3 and 7 days after. We send polite emails and stop once the invoice is paid.

Can my clients pay online?

Yes. Every invoice has a secure payment link that supports cards and bank transfers.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, the Free plan includes 5 invoices per month with no credit card required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, and you can export all your data.
