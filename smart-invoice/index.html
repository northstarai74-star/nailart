    Questions

    Before you start

    How do follow-ups work?

    You choose a schedule, for example 3 days before the due date, on the day, and 3 and 7 days after. Each message goes out at the time you set and stops once the invoice is paid.

    Can clients pay online?

    Yes. Every invoice has a payment link that accepts cards and bank transfers.

    What does the free plan include?

    Five invoices a month with basic reminders and PDF export. No card needed.

    Can I export my data?

    Yes. Export all invoices and client records as CSV or PDF at any time.

    Get your next invoice paid sooner

    Create your account in two minutes. Your first five invoices each month are free.
