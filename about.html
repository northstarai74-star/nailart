About the studio

Started in a spare room. Still run the same way.

Lacque & Co. opened as a single desk in 2016. Twelve years on, we're still deliberately small — two chairs, one classroom, and a waiting list we're not in a hurry to clear.

Our story

Twelve years, one waiting list.

Nia Fairweather started Lacque & Co. after five years working salon floors that valued speed over structure. The studio was built on a simple rule: every set gets the time it needs, and every technique gets taught properly or not at all.

The academy followed in 2019, after too many artists asked Nia to teach them what her clients kept complimenting. We still cap every cohort at six for the same reason we still run two chairs — quality drops the moment attention splits.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't bend.

01

Prep is not optional

Every set starts with a proper dry prep. It's the least visible part of the work and the reason our sets outlast most.

02

Small rooms, honest attention

Two chairs. Six students. We turn away bookings before we compromise on either.

03

Nail health over nail length

We'll tell you when a shape or length isn't right for your natural plate — even if you didn't ask.

04

Teach it the way we do it

Nothing in the academy syllabus is a shortcut we wouldn't use on a paying client ourselves.
