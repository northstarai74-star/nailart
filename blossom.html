About the studio

A small studio, one chair, full attention.

Blossom keeps one appointment at a time so every set gets the time it actually needs — no double-booking, no rush between clients. It's a slower way to run a studio, and it shows in how long the sets last.

  • One artist, one client — no overlap
  • Dry, damage-free prep on every visit
  • Custom art mixed to your reference, not a menu
  • Sets built to last three to four weeks
