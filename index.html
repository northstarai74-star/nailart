The academy

You will not learn this from a ring light.

Our cohorts cap at six. You practise on tips for one week and on live models for the rest, with a tutor at your shoulder correcting the angle of your e-file bit before it becomes a habit.

  • Six students per cohort — the tutor sees every hand, every day.
  • A full professional kit included — e-file, bits, gels, brushes, lamp.
  • Live models from week two, sourced and booked by the studio.
  • Twelve months of mentoring — monthly clinics and a private critique channel.
  • Insurable certification recognised by Irish and UK insurers.
